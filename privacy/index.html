Marker Walk Privacy Policy

Last updated November 2026

The short version

Marker Walk has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not collect personal information, and it does not send your location anywhere.

Location

With your permission, Marker Walk uses your iPhone's location only while the app is open. It is used to find places near you, to guide you to a place in Surprise me, and to tell which U.S. state you are in. This happens on your iPhone, using state borders that are built into the app. Your location is never sent to the developer or to any server, and it is not stored.

Downloads

When you download a state's places, the app requests one file from a static web server (GitHub Pages). That server can see the usual technical details of any web request, such as your IP address, and which state file was requested. It never receives your location. The developer does not run any server that keeps this information.

Data stored on your device

Places you save, mark as visited or browse, your settings, and the downloaded state stay on your iPhone. They are not uploaded. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

The optional "All states" purchase is handled entirely by Apple. The developer receives no payment details and no personal information. Apple's privacy policy applies to the purchase.

Third parties

Marker Walk contains no third-party SDKs, advertising or analytics. Place information comes from public data sets (the National Register of Historic Places, Wikipedia and Wikidata, and the U.S. Census Bureau) that were prepared in advance and are downloaded as static files.

Children

Marker Walk collects no personal information from anyone, including children. Children should use it with an adult, as places are outdoors.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
