Marker Walk Support

Help for the Marker Walk iPhone app

Get help or report a problem

Open an issue on GitHub. Please say which iPhone and iOS version you have and what happened. Please do not post personal information or your exact location.

Common questions

How do I download a different state?

Go to Settings, then Places data, then Downloaded state. One state is kept on your iPhone at a time, so downloading another replaces it. The state you are in is free. Other states need the one-time "All states" purchase.

I bought "All states" but it is locked.

Go to Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it. Refunds and purchase problems are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

The places on my phone are out of date.

Open Settings, then Places data, then Downloaded state. If an update is available, an Update button appears under "On this iPhone".

A place is wrong, closed or missing.

Place information comes from Wikipedia, Wikidata and the National Register of Historic Places, and can be out of date. You can improve it for everyone by editing the place's Wikipedia or Wikidata entry. You are welcome to report it here too.

The arrow is not pointing the right way.

Hold your phone flat and away from metal or magnets, and move it in a figure eight to calibrate the compass. Check that Location is on for Marker Walk in iPhone Settings. The arrow points in a straight line to the place, not along a route, so use sidewalks and crossings and look up while you walk.
